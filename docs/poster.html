Cohort

A job application tracker with interview preparation and privacy-preserving federated learning.

Problem

A student applying for jobs keeps the state of the search in a spreadsheet, a notes app and memory.

  • Applications stall and nothing points them out.
  • What was asked in an interview is forgotten within days.
  • Preparation follows a generic question list, not the topics that went badly.
  • Learning from other candidates needs data that is personal and should not be shared.

What Cohort does

  • Tracks applications with a status workflow and a full history.
  • Records interviews, questions and a short debrief.
  • Scores topic mastery and recommends what to practise next, with the evidence shown.
  • Trains a shared model by federated learning: only clipped, noised updates leave a user's partition, and a privacy ledger lists them.
  • Helps with the writing around a search on a local AI model: resume and job description match, interview prep packs and email drafts.

Flow

  1. Add an application and move it through its stages.
  2. Schedule interviews and write a debrief afterwards.
  3. Questions go into the question bank and weak topics show up in the mastery graph.
  4. Recommendations suggest practice, follow-ups and stalled applications.
  5. Analytics show how the search is going, with sample sizes.

Main features

FeatureDetail
Status workflowIllegal moves are refused and every change is kept in the history.
Data isolationAnother user's record returns 404, not 403.
Mastery graphTopics have prerequisites; mastery decays over time.
Stall detectionUses the user's own median time in a stage.
RecommendationsEach one has an evidence tier and a reason.
Privacy ledgerLists every federated round the user took part in.
PracticeQuestion bank, spaced review queue, mock interviews and a STAR story bank.
